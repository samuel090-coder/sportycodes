## 📋 Next Steps: Submit to Google & Bing

### **Step 1: Google Search Console Setup**

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console

2. **Add Your Property**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Enter your domain: `yourdomain.com`

3. **Verify Ownership (Choose One Method)**
   
   **Method A: HTML Meta Tag (Recommended)**
   - Google will give you a verification code like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="YOUR_CODE" />`
   - Copy the code (just the content value)
   - Go to Admin Panel → SEO Manager
   - Paste it in "Google Verification Code"
   - Click "Save SEO Settings"
   - Return to Google Search Console and click "Verify"

   **Method B: HTML File Upload**
   - Download the verification file from Google
   - Upload it to your site's `public/` folder
   - Click "Verify"

4. **Submit Your Sitemap**
   - In Google Search Console, click "Sitemaps"
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
   - ✅ Google will now automatically index all your pages

---

### **Step 2: Bing Webmaster Tools Setup**

1. **Go to Bing Webmaster Tools**
   - Visit: https://www.bing.com/webmasters

2. **Add Your Site**
   - Click "Add a site"
   - Enter: `yourdomain.com`

3. **Verify Ownership**
   - Choose "Meta tag" method
   - Copy the verification code
   - Go to Admin Panel → SEO Manager
   - Paste it in "Bing Verification Code"
   - Click "Save SEO Settings"
   - Return to Bing and click "Verify"

4. **Submit Your Sitemap**
   - Go to "Sitemaps" section
   - Enter: `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
